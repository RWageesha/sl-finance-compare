Verification & Standards

1

Data Sources

  • Official commercial bank websites operating in Sri Lanka.
  • Official documents and public press releases issued by registered financial institutions.
  • Published datasets and statistical summaries from the Central Bank of Sri Lanka (CBSL), where available.
2

How Often Is Data Checked?

Product CategoryCheck Frequency
Fixed Deposit RatesEvery 6 hours
Savings Account RatesEvery 6 hours
Personal & Housing Loan RatesEvery 6 hours
3

What Does Verified Mean?

Every rate shown carries the timestamp of the scrape that captured it, straight from the bank's own published page — nothing is manually typed in or estimated.

4

Data Limitations

Rates change frequently and banks may offer preferential terms not reflected here. All figures should be treated as indicative — always confirm directly with the bank before making a decision.

Methodology

1

Collection

Automated scraping of official bank websites and published rate sheets. Never third-party aggregators or user-submitted data.

2

Validation

Range checks flag unusual values before publishing. Out-of-range or malformed scrapes are quarantined for manual review.

3

Verification

Every published rate carries a timestamp of when it was last confirmed against the source. Stale rates are clearly marked.

4

Publishing

Rates appear on the site only after passing all checks. Accurate and honest data over completeness.

Core Sourcing Principle: No invented data, anywhere. If we do not have verified data for a specific bank or tenure, we explicitly say so. We never fill gaps with placeholder values.
